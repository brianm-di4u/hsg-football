# Advocate rebuttal: loop 14, High tier

All paths are relative to `games/football/screenshots/review-loop-14/`. I opened every capture cited below. For dense frames I made zoomed crops (strip frames at 2×, the R05 line, the R12 pile, the R14 backdrop, the R15 action area).

Critic means: art 6.25, lighting 6.5, animation 5.5, UI 6.75, typography 7.0, readability 6.25, mobile 6.25, authenticity 6.25.

## Summary
Most of both critiques holds up. These points are real:
- The end zones open onto a void (`high/desktop/R14`).
- Players have no faces and smooth pants.
- Bodies in the tackle pile interpenetrate.
- Portrait pre-snap framing clips receivers (`medium/mobile/R04`).
- The R15 replay is blurred.

Both critics also make factual errors. Critic A blames a cloned pose on the wrong team and wrong phase of play. A also calls the end zone a void in a capture that shows a field house, and says there is no camera push-in when one is visible. Codex calls the toast's coverage of R12 heavier than it is, misses a visible wrap arm, and asks for rain splashes the build already draws. Codex's animation score of 5.0 rests on the rubric's 5 anchor ("stiff falls, floaty contact"), but this loop's captures show multi-body falls to the ground.

## Category by category

### Art (A 6.5, Codex 6.0; mean 6.25). Proposed: 6.5
**Conceded:**
- No face is readable behind any cage (`crops/high/R07-slot_QB`, `crops/high/R14-carrier`).
- The Lakeside arms are flat pale grey (`crops/high/R06-slot_LG`).
- The stance pants are a smooth orange shell with no folds.
- Codex's "toy-like" shoulder-to-waist taper is visible in `crops/high/R04-slot_QB`.

**Rebutted or qualified:**
- **"Mitten hands" (A) and "crude gloves" (Codex) are overstated.** In `crops/high/R06-slot_C-side` the near player's hands have a separate thumb, and the right hand shows separated finger segments. That is crude, but it is not a mitten.
- **Material contrast is clearly met.** Every close-up shows glossy helmets with a specular hotspot, a decal and a cage against matte jerseys with sleeve stripes and outlined numbers (`crops/high/R06-slot_C-side`, `crops/high/R14-carrier`, `crops/high/R09-slot_Z`). This is an explicit rubric-7 criterion, and Codex's 6.0 does not credit it.
- **Crowd.** Codex says the crowd has "conspicuously repeated silhouettes", but `crops/high/R01-crowd` shows at least five poses (arms up, one arm up, arms crossed, hands to face, arms down), three shirt colours and varied skin tones. The body mesh repeats; the silhouettes are not uniform.
- **The oversized ball (A).** A deducts it under art, then credits the "oversized ball" in readability. It is a deliberate readability aid, as in retrobowl-1, and shouldn't count against art.
- **Venue.** A's claim that the venue has "no structures" is contradicted by `high/desktop/R03`: a field house with lit windows and a gold fascia band stands behind the end zone.

The rubric-7 checks are mixed. Gear and the helmet/jersey contrast pass. Turf texture passes (`crops/high/R04-turf_26_67_42`), and the crowd reads as people. Faces and cloth fail. 6.5 is the fair score.

### Lighting (A 6.5, Codex 6.5; mean 6.5). Proposed: 6.5
**Conceded:**
- Each player has one dominant shadow (`high/desktop/R09`, `high/desktop/R08`).
- The wet sheen is a local hotspot (`crops/high/R18-turf_26_67_40`).
- The surround behind the end zones is unlit (`high/desktop/R14`).
- **Codex's R15 blur point is correct.** In my zoom of `high/desktop/R15`, the carrier (#44) and every player near him are soft, not just the background.

**Qualified:**
- Rain is more than "straight streaks". `crops/high/R18-turf_26_67_40` shows splash dots at the players' feet and a specular hotspot. `high/desktop/R18` shows splash flecks around the backfield.
- `high/desktop/R01` and `high/desktop/R20` show real lamp bloom, believable night exposure, and a field brighter than the stands.
- The day/mist variant also re-themes the HUD to a light sidebar (`high/desktop/R19`). It is an intentional grade, not a broken flat wash, though A is right that contrast on the field is low.

No change: 6.5.

### Animation (A 6.0, Codex 5.0; mean 5.5). Proposed: 6.0
**Conceded:**
- Bodies in the pile interpenetrate: in `crops/high/R12-carrier`, the white torso of #47 merges into the blue #20.
- The players standing near the whistle are in neutral stances (`high/desktop/R12`).
- There is no visible particle effect at contact in any strip.
- The sweep runners stay fairly upright (`strips/high/R11`).

**Rebutted:**
- **A blames the wrong team and the wrong phase for "four defenders… identical hands-on-knees crouch" (`strips/high/R10` frames 6–8).** `high/desktop/R10` shows Hot Springs (black) on offense, with #44 Delacroix carrying. The crouched players are black-jersey Hot Springs blockers, and they hold the same squat in frames 1–5 of the live play. The defect is a frozen blocking pose that persists after the whistle, not a cloned post-play idle. That moves the fix toward block-release and engagement reactions (WS4) rather than an idle-pose pool.
- **"#56 hangs airborne over the pile" (A) is not supported.** In my crop of `crops/high/R12-carrier`, #56's rear foot sits on the same turf baseline as the umpire's feet at the same depth. He is standing behind the pile in a striding, leaning pose, not floating.
- **"Nearly every celebrant does the same two-arms-up pose" (A) is overstated.** `crops/high/R14-carrier` shows four distinct poses:
  - #74: both hands overhead.
  - #12: one arm up, ball held low in the other.
  - #9: arms in a V.
  - The rear player: arm extended low, and #69 on the right reaching forward.
- **"Convergence without a convincing wrap" (Codex) is contradicted by `crops/high/R13-carrier`.** The tackler's dark arm crosses #20's back at the waist. That is a visible wrap. Body overlap is still a fair criticism.
- **"No camera punch-in in any strip" (A) is wrong.** In `strips/high/R09`, frame 3 to frame 4, the camera tightens noticeably on the receiver at the catch: the scoreboard and yard lines grow and the receiver's raised arms fill more of the frame. Slow motion cannot be judged from stills either way.
- **QB "arm a stiff raised block" (A).** `crops/high/R07-slot_QB` shows the ball cocked behind the helmet, elbow bent and up: a textbook loaded throwing position. A's point that this is illegible at the live camera distance (`strips/high/R07`) stands.
- **Codex's 5.0 matches the rubric's 5 anchor ("stiff falls"), which no longer describes the build.** `strips/high/R13` frames 7–8 and `crops/high/R12-carrier` show the carrier and two or three tacklers going to the ground in staggered order. The sweep gait has knee lift (`strips/high/R11`), and the catch has a reach (`strips/high/R09` frame 4).

6.0 sits at the top of the allowed band and is supported. It is not a 7: there are no particles, the pile overlaps, and the blocking freezes.
