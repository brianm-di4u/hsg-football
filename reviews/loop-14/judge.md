# Judge ruling: loop 14, High tier

All capture paths are relative to `games/football/screenshots/review-loop-14/`. I opened every capture cited below, and I made zoomed crops of the R10 strip and frame, the R12 pile, the R13 close-up and strip, and the landscape R22 list. Those crops are in the judge14 scratch folder.

**Verdict: continue.** Animation, art, lighting, readability and mobile are all below the 7 gate. UI, typography and authenticity meet their gates.

## Disputed facts, checked against the captures

1. **Who the crouching players in `strips/high/R10` are.** Neither critic nor the advocate has it fully right.
   - The crouched figures in frames 6–8 have black jerseys with gold numbers, so they are Hot Springs blockers. My 3× zoom of `high/desktop/R10` (the same play, around frame 1) shows that each one is fused with a Lakeside defender: a blue helmet and a white sleeve sit inside the same silhouette as the black jersey.
   - These pairs hold the same engaged squat in the same spots from the snap through the whistle.
   - So A is wrong that these are "four defenders" doing a post-play idle. The advocate is right that it is a frozen block engagement. But the defect is worse than the advocate says: the engaged pairs interpenetrate, which is an animation and contact problem, not only a pose problem.
2. **Whether #56 is airborne in `crops/high/R12-carrier`.** A's "airborne" is not supported. The advocate's claim that his foot is on the umpire's baseline isn't visible either. #56 is frozen mid-stride, and his lower legs disappear into the pile bodies: his feet are hidden and his shins clip into the pile. I rule it as clipping, not floating.
3. **The celebration poses in `crops/high/R14-carrier`.** The advocate is right that there are at least four distinct poses: #74 with both hands up, #12 with one arm up and the ball held low, #9 with arms in a V, and #69 reaching forward. A's "nearly every celebrant" is overstated. Even so, in `high/desktop/R14` about 7 of 11 players have both arms raised, so variety is limited, not absent.
4. **Whether R09 shows a camera push-in.** It does. In `strips/high/R09`, from frame 3 to frame 4 the framing tightens markedly: the midfield logo is about 1.5× wider, and the scoreboard and receivers are larger. The advocate is right and A is wrong. From stills I can't tell whether this is an eased push or a hard reframe.
5. **The wrap in `crops/high/R13-carrier`.** There is no wrap. In my zoom, #20's own left arm hangs at his side. The only dark arm hangs vertically from a tackler to the right and doesn't touch #20's back. A black Hot Springs helmet sits inside a blue Lakeside jersey: two bodies merged.
   - Codex is right and the advocate is wrong.
   - `strips/high/R13` frames 7–8 do show the carrier going down with a white-jersey body on the turf, so the pile itself is real.
6. **End-zone surrounds.**
   - `high/desktop/R03` shows a field house with lit windows and a gold fascia behind the end zone, so A's "every end zone" is wrong.
   - `high/desktop/R14` is a flat olive plane and a dark wall: a void.
   - `high/desktop/R20` is a flat grey-blue plane with only the scoreboard: no track, fence or structures.
   - So A is right on R14 and R20 and wrong on R03.
   - The advocate's track apron is real: a tan strip runs along the home sideline in `high/desktop/R01`.
7. **How much of `high/desktop/R12` the toast covers.** The toast's top edge is at y≈643. The pile bodies sit at y≈605–640 and are visible above it. The toast covers the bottom of the pile, the feet, and the near-foreground players and official. The advocate and A are accurate; Codex's "much of the tackle aftermath" is overstated.
8. **The scroll cue in `medium/landscape/R22`.** It exists, but it is minimal. About the top 10 CSS px of card 7 shows, with dimmed text. There is no scrollbar and no other indicator. The advocate is technically right and Codex's "no obvious cue" is a fair judgement. Six of ten plays are visible, but this isn't worth a gap of its own.
9. **Other facts.** Rain splash dots are present (`crops/high/R18-turf_26_67_40`), so Codex's "introduce splashes" is already done. The R15 replay is soft on the carrier as well as the background (`high/desktop/R15`), so Codex is right on that.

## Category rulings

### Art: 6.5 (A 6.5, Codex 6.0)
- **What reaches the 7 anchor.**
  - Helmet and jersey materials contrast clearly: glossy shells with a hotspot, decals and cages against matte jerseys with sleeve stripes and outlined numbers (`crops/high/R06-slot_C-side`, `crops/high/R04-slot_QB`).
  - The turf has grain at field level (`crops/high/R18-turf_26_67_40`).
  - The crowd reads as people, with varied poses and shirt colours (`crops/high/R01-crowd`).
  - `high/desktop/R03` has a built field house.
- **What falls short.**
  - Lakeside arms are flat grey mannequin limbs, and the hands are crude splayed shapes rather than mittens (`crops/high/R06-slot_C-side`).
  - No faces are visible behind any cage.
  - Players have toy-like shoulder-to-waist tapers and smooth pants with no folds (`crops/high/R04-slot_QB`).
  - The end-zone surround is a void in `high/desktop/R14`.
- **Ruling.** Codex's 6.0 doesn't credit the met material-contrast criterion; A's 6.5 holds. Below axis-3, which has real bodies and cloth.

### Lighting & atmosphere: 6.5 (both 6.5)
- **What works.** Night exposure, lamp bloom and the bright field against darker stands are convincing (`high/desktop/R01`, `high/desktop/R20`).
- **What falls short.**
  - Each player has one dominant shadow (`high/desktop/R09`).
  - The rain wet sheen is a local hotspot plus splash dots, not a wet field (`high/desktop/R18`, `crops/high/R18-turf_26_67_40`).
  - The end-zone surround is unlit (`high/desktop/R14`).
  - The replay blur softens the carrier (`high/desktop/R15`).
- **Ruling.** Below cfb26-3.

### Animation & game feel: 5.5 (A 6.0, Codex 5.0)
The critics are 1.0 apart. I checked `strips/high/R10`, `strips/high/R13`, `strips/high/R09`, `strips/high/R11` and `strips/high/R07`, plus the R12 and R13 close-ups.
- **For A and the advocate.**
  - Tackles now end on the ground with more than one body (`strips/high/R13` frames 7–8, `crops/high/R12-carrier`).
  - There is a camera tighten at the catch (`strips/high/R09` frames 3→4).
  - The QB close-up shows a legible loaded arm (`crops/high/R07-slot_QB`).
  - Celebrations use at least four poses.
- **For Codex.**
  - The R13 contact shows no wrap and merged bodies (`crops/high/R13-carrier`).
  - Pile bodies and #56's legs interpenetrate (`crops/high/R12-carrier`).
  - Every engaged blocking pair in `strips/high/R10` is fused into one silhouette and frozen in the same squat from snap to whistle.
  - The sweep runners stay upright, with no visible cut plant (`strips/high/R11`).
  - There are no contact particles, and the throw is illegible at the live camera distance (`strips/high/R07`).
- **Ruling.** The build is past the 5 anchor's "stiff falls", but contact still has bodies sharing volume in every contact capture. That fails the 7 anchor's planted, legible contact. Neither critic is fully right: A missed the fused blocking pairs and the missing wrap; Codex missed the push-in and the multi-body falls. 5.5.

### UI / HUD: 7.0 (A 7.0, Codex 6.5)
- **What works.**
  - The scorebug is broadcast-style.
  - Pre-snap identification is strong: dashed routes, five shaped receiver badges and a turf down stencil (`high/desktop/R04`).
  - The new on-field defensive call draws (`high/desktop/R05`).
  - The result toast has a clear two-level hierarchy (`high/desktop/R21`).
- **Deductions.**
  - The toast covers the pile's base and the front celebrants' legs (`high/desktop/R12`, `high/desktop/R14`).
  - The R05 rush arrows bunch into one knot with an official standing inside it.
- **Ruling.** The overlaps are minor and on the edges of the action, not over the focal point. That keeps the score at the anchor, not above it. Codex's sidebar complaint is a layout choice; nothing in the sidebar is stale.

### Typography: 7.0 (both 7.0)
A consistent condensed display face is paired with a humanist sans. The numerals are clear and the hierarchy reads ("+7 YD · FIRST DOWN" over "M. DELACROIX RUN") (`high/desktop/R21`, `high/desktop/R22`, `medium/mobile/R22`). The micro-labels ("HOME", the "Space" key cap) are about 8–9 px, which is the remaining refinement.

### Readability: 6.5 (A 6.5, Codex 6.0)
- **What reads.**
  - Ball, trail, landing ring and target badge read at a glance (`high/desktop/R09`, `high/desktop/R08`).
  - The carrier's ring and tag work on both viewports (`high/desktop/R10`, `medium/mobile/R10`).
  - The black jerseys separate well from the turf, so A's "only findable by tag" is overstated.
- **What doesn't.**
  - Players are about 30 px tall at the default desktop camera (`high/desktop/R04`), and about 20 px in the R03 wide shot.
  - In `medium/mobile/R04`, receiver 1 is at the left edge with its badge over the "1ST" of the stencil, and receiver 2 is half off the right edge. Both carry edge pointers, so the targets are signposted but not framed.
- **Ruling.** 6.5. Codex overstates the information loss.

### Mobile: 6.0 (A 6.5, Codex 6.0)
The 7 anchor has three items, and two are short:
- **Figures.** Portrait figures are about 25 CSS px (`medium/mobile/R10`).
- **Portrait framing.** Portrait pass pre-snap framing is not intentional: the outside receivers are clipped (`medium/mobile/R04`).
- **Performance.** This one is met, narrowly: Medium mobile under the 4× throttle has a 37 fps minimum, with a 34.0 ms worst p95.

The HUD is thumb-friendly: full-width JUKE, HIKE and CHANGE PLAY, and large play cards (`medium/mobile/R22`). Landscape fits the whole formation (`medium/landscape/R04`), but its play list shows 6 of 10 cards with only a sliver cue (`medium/landscape/R22`). Below retrobowl-1, which frames the whole formation at phone width.

- **What lands.**
  - A packed, raked home grandstand, light towers, a track apron along the home sideline and an HSHS/LHS scoreboard (`high/desktop/R01`, `crops/high/R01-board`).
  - A field house behind the R03 end zone (`high/desktop/R03`).
  - A distinct 3A Jessieville venue (`high/desktop/R20`).
  - H-style padded uprights (`high/desktop/R14`), local play names and loose sideline clusters (`high/desktop/R15`).
- **What's missing.**
  - No band, cheer line or student-section signage in any capture.
  - The end-zone surround is a void in R14 and R20.
  - The pines still form a solid wall that dwarfs the visitor bleacher (`high/desktop/R15`).
- **Ruling.** Above the gate, below cfb26-1's community staging.

## Ranked gaps (gain ÷ effort; S=1, M=2, L=3)

1. **G-read-camera-scale** (readability, WS7, S, +0.4, severity 3)
   - **Problem:** players are about 30 px tall at the default desktop camera (`high/desktop/R04`, `high/desktop/R05`).
   - **Fix:** move the default behind-offense camera in and down so the line is about 45 px tall at 1280×800, keeping all eleven offensive players and the route badges inside the frame (as in R04).
2. **G-ui-toast-overlap** (ui, WS7, S, +0.3, severity 2). Merges G-action-space.
   - **Problem:** the toast covers the pile base and the celebrants' legs (`high/desktop/R12`, `high/desktop/R14`).
   - **Fix:** when a result toast shows, frame the post-play camera so the ball spot sits in the upper 55% of the field view, or dock the toast under the scorebug. Test: no player's bounding box intersects the toast in R12 and R14.
3. **G-contact-weight** (animation, WS4b, M, +0.5, severity 5). Merges G-anim-pose-variety.
   - **Problem:** at contact and in the pile, bodies share volume (`crops/high/R13-carrier`, `crops/high/R12-carrier`, `strips/high/R13`).
   - **Fix:** in tackles and piles:
     - run a capsule push-out (torso radius about 0.35 m) between all players each tick, so no two torsos overlap;
     - stack the 2nd and 3rd tacklers on top of the carrier with a vertical offset, not inside him;
     - use IK to close the first tackler's arms around the carrier's waist on the contact frame;
     - keep standing players' feet outside the pile footprint;
     - draw celebrations from a pool of 5–6 poses with mirroring and a cap of 40% on both-arms-up.
   - **Test:** R12 and R13 close-ups show separate silhouettes and one visible wrap arm.
4. **G-mobile-portrait-framing** (mobile, WS7, M, +0.5, severity 4). Merges G-portrait-coverage.
   - **Problem:** portrait pre-snap clips the outside receivers (`medium/mobile/R04`, `high/mobile/R04`).
   - **Fix:** fit the portrait pre-snap camera to the formation's width plus route extents plus a 24 CSS px margin, then pull in after the snap. Offset the down-and-distance stencil when a receiver badge overlaps it.
   - **Test:** all five badges are fully on screen in `medium/mobile/R04`.
5. **G-gait-and-blocking** (animation, WS4, M, +0.5, severity 4). The overlapping parts of G-anim-feel-beats are moved here.
   - **Problem:** engaged blocking pairs are fused and frozen in one squat from snap to whistle (`strips/high/R10`, `high/desktop/R10`).
   - **Fix:**
     - hold engaged blockers and defenders face to face 0.6–0.8 m apart, with hands on each other's chest and a drive or shed animation that moves them;
     - release them to upright jog or turn-and-watch poses at the whistle;
     - add a planted outside-foot cut frame with 10–15° torso lean on the sweep (`strips/high/R11`).
6. **G-venue-endzone-void** (authenticity, WS2, M, +0.4, severity 3)
   - **Problem:** the surround behind the end zone is empty in R14 and R20 (`high/desktop/R14`, `high/desktop/R20`). R03 already has a field house.
   - **Fix:**
     - continue the track around the end zones;
     - add a chain-link fence at the end line plus 10 yd;
     - add a berm or field-house block and two parking-lot light poles beyond the fence;
     - thin the visitor-side pine wall so the tree tops sit below the light-tower height (`high/desktop/R15`).
7. **G-community-staging** (authenticity, WS2, M, +0.4, severity 3). Takes the band and student parts that overlapped G-venue-endzone-void.
   - **Problem:** no band, student section or cheer line in any capture (`crops/high/R01-crowd`, `high/desktop/R01`).
   - **Fix:** add a uniformed band block with instrument silhouettes in one home-stand section, a student section with painted shirts and 3–4 sign boards, and a cheer line on the track in front of the home stand.
8. **G-light-multishadow-wet** (lighting, WS1, M, +0.4, severity 3). Merges G-wet-surface; splashes already exist, so they are not part of the fix.
   - **Problem:** one shadow per player, a hotspot-only rain sheen, and unlit stand faces and end-zone surround (`high/desktop/R09`, `crops/high/R18-turf_26_67_40`, `high/desktop/R14`).
   - **Fix:**
     - add 2–3 faint secondary shadow lobes toward the other towers;
     - in rain, lower roughness across the whole field, with specular streaks toward each tower;
     - add tower spill on the stand faces and the end-zone surround.
9. **G-replay-blur** (lighting, WS1, S, +0.2, severity 2)
   - **Problem:** the replay blur softens the carrier, not just the background (`high/desktop/R15`).
   - **Fix:** put the replay depth-of-field focus on the carrier (focus distance equals the camera-to-carrier distance) and limit blur to more than 15 yd from him.
   - **Test:** #44 and his tag are sharp in R15.
10. **G-def-call-legibility** (ui, WS7, S, +0.2, severity 2)
    - **Problem:** the rush arrows bunch into a single knot over the line with an official inside it (`high/desktop/R05`).
    - **Fix:** fan the arrows out by gap, starting each arrow at its rusher's helmet; label the zone ovals with short names (FLAT, HOOK, DEEP ½); move the umpire 3 yd off the ball pre-snap when a defensive diagram is shown.
11. **G-art-limbs-faces** (art, WS3, L, +0.5, severity 4). Merges G-player-anatomy.
    - **Problem:** grey mannequin arms, crude hands, no faces, toy torso taper and smooth pants (`crops/high/R06-slot_C-side`, `crops/high/R04-slot_QB`).
    - **Fix:**
      - skin-tone forearms with sleeve and elbow-pad seams;
      - split-finger gloves;
      - a face plane with brow shadow behind the cage;
      - a softer shoulder-to-torso transition and two or three body-type scales;
      - normal and AO fold maps on the jersey and pants.
12. **G-anim-feel-beats** (animation, WS4, M, +0.3, severity 3)
    - **Problem:** no particles at contact and no slow motion at the tackle (`strips/high/R13`, `strips/high/R09`). The catch push-in already exists.
    - **Fix:** keep the catch push-in; add a turf-spray and dust burst at the first tackle-contact point, plus a 0.25 s ease to 0.4× time scale with a 10% dolly-in at first contact.

Dropped from the top 12: **G-mobile-perf-p95** (+0.2, M). The minimum fps clears 30 under the 4× throttle, and the p95 misses the 33.3 ms budget by only 0.7 ms.
