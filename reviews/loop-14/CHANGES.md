Changes since loop 13 (facts only). Score **High** on desktop. Medium is the phone and mid tier. Low is the v2 look, captured to catch regressions.

- **Defense pre-snap, on the field.** When you're on defense, your call is drawn on the turf before the snap (as the offense's routes are):
  - red rush arrows from every rusher into the backfield;
  - shaded blue ovals at each zone defender's landmark, with a dashed line from the defender to it;
  - dashed yellow lines from each man defender to his receiver;
  - a yellow ring around a spy.
- **Tackles.** Up to two more defenders who were within about 2.4 yd of the carrier at the whistle now go down with him, a beat apart (0.12 s each), so stops end in a pile on the ground rather than one fall in an upright cluster.
- **Venue (all tiers).**
  - The pines behind the visitor stands are set back further (16–56 yd behind the stand, was 6–40), slightly smaller, and built from two tiers of foliage, so they read as a tree line rather than spikes.
  - The sideline teams stand in loose clusters of four with varied depth and facing, not an evenly spaced row.
