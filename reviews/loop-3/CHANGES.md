Changes since loop 2 (facts only). Score **High** on desktop; Medium is the phone/mid tier; Low is the v2 look and is captured for regressions.

- **Gait (all tiers).** The stride phase now advances with the distance each player actually covers, with step length matched to the leg swing. Replays follow the same strides.
- **Feet (all tiers).** Two-bone leg IK puts the supporting foot's sole on the turf whenever it would sink or hover near the ground, in stances, idles and runs. It is off during falls, kicks and celebration hops.
- **Throw (all tiers).** A keyed four-beat clip from the release tick: arm cocked with the shoulders closed and the front foot stepping, through over the top, follow-through across the body, then settle. It includes shoulder-hip twist.
  - While reading the field, the passer holds the ball high in both hands.
  - A released pass starts about 2 yd up at the hand (it used to start at the turf) and eases into its flight.
- **Tackles (all tiers).** The tackler wraps, arms forward and closing around the carrier, instead of a superman dive.
- **Carrier ID tag.** It follows the rendered player's helmet each frame and disappears when the whistle blows.
- **High.** Haze cones and the light-spill dome are dimmer, and bloom is softer and higher-threshold, so the night above the lamps stays darker.
- **Phone cameras.** The pre-snap and live cameras are pitched further down, so the field starts right under the HUD.
- **Unchanged since loop 2:** player and crowd assets, lighting setup, HUD layout and type, venue geometry.
