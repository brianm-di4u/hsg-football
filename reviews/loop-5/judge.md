Capture paths are relative to `games/football/screenshots/review-loop-5/`.

- **Close-ups:** `crops/high/R03-slot_QB`, `R04-slot_QB`, `R06-slot_C-side`, `R10-carrier`, `R12-carrier`, `R18-turf_26_67_40`, `R01-board`.
- **Motion strips:** `strips/high/R09`, `R10`, `R11`, `R13`.

## Where the critics disagreed

No category has a critic spread above 1.0; the largest is 0.5. Factual disputes I settled from the captures:
- **Clock gap in the strips: the advocate is right.** The board clock reads 3:20 in frames 1–5 and 3:18 in frame 6 of both `strips/high/R09` and `strips/high/R10`. The strips skip the catch-to-ground and tackle moments, so they cannot prove a pose snap. They do prove the catch and fall are never shown, and that still fails the 7 anchor's "legible catch and tackle beats".
- **Ball in flight: the advocate is right.** In `high/desktop/R08` the ball is at about (550, 475) over open turf, clear of every jersey. Critic A's "dark blob over black jerseys" holds only for the close-up.
- **Seven-segment "1": the advocate is right.** `crops/high/R01-board` draws the "1" on the right-hand segments, the same in the clock, QTR and DOWN cells. That is authentic, not a spacing defect.
- **Desktop result bands: the advocate is right.** In `high/desktop/R12` and `high/desktop/R21` the band sits below the pile and the huddle. On the phone, **Critic A is right**:
  - `high/mobile/R12` frames empty midfield, and the pile is hidden behind the band.
  - In `high/mobile/R21` the band covers the offense.
  - In `high/landscape/R14` the touchdown banner covers the celebration.
- **Wet turf: both critics are partly right.** At gameplay distance the turf looks dry (`high/desktop/R18`, Critic A). In the close-up `crops/high/R18-turf_26_67_40` the "sheen" is glitter-like noise patches plus hard-edged square splash quads. That supports Critic B's "aggressive sparkle", and it is still not a convincing wet surface.
- **New finding: the game clock disagrees.** In `high/desktop/R12` and `high/desktop/R13` the scorebug reads 3:18, while the stadium board and the play log read 3:17. This is a small stale-data inconsistency.

## Rulings by category

### Art: 6.0
- **Meets parts of the 7 anchor.** The new model clearly clears the 5 anchor. In `crops/high/R03-slot_QB` glossy copper helmets with real hot spots sit next to matte jerseys, and the numbers, pant stripes and sock stripes are crisp. Kits differ by school (`crops/high/R04-slot_QB`, `crops/high/R06-slot_C-side`). The crowd reads as people in school colours (`crops/high/R04-slot_QB`, background).
- **Close-ups fail the 7 anchor.**
  - Every player has the same balloon-shaped seat and thighs (`crops/high/R04-slot_QB` #12 and #44, `crops/high/R10-carrier` #66, #74 and #69).
  - Gloves are splayed rakes, and #65's hem is torn into sawteeth (`crops/high/R06-slot_C-side`).
  - The ball floats above a pile of interpenetrating bodies (`crops/high/R12-carrier`).
  - The official in the foreground of `crops/high/R03-slot_QB` is a capsule with a blank head and sleeve tubes floating off the torso.
- **Benchmark.** Below `axis-3.jpg`'s anatomy and cloth.
- **Verdict.** I side with Critic B and the advocate: material contrast earns the half point over 5.5, and the close-up defects keep it well off 7.

### Lighting & atmosphere: 6.0
- **What works.** `high/desktop/R04` and `high/desktop/R20` show a believable night grade, lamp bloom and per-player shadows.
- **What fails.** The 7 anchor's "wet sheen in rain" fails at gameplay distance (`high/desktop/R18`), and the close-up wet response is noise, not reflection (`crops/high/R18-turf_26_67_40`).
  - The establishing frame `high/desktop/R01` is a near-black, flat bowl with no falloff into the stands.
  - The sideline replay has a dark vertical smudge at bottom centre (`high/desktop/R13`).
- **Verdict.** The lighting is unchanged this loop, and nothing on screen moves it. I side with Critic A over the 6.5 from Critic B and the advocate.

### Animation & game feel: 5.0
- **Strips.**
  - `strips/high/R13` holds the carrier and tackler wrapped and upright for all 8 replay frames, with no drive and no fall.
  - `strips/high/R09` never shows the catch: frames 1–3 have the ball in the air, frame 4 is a punch-in with the receiver already running, and then the 2 s gap.
  - `strips/high/R11` shows lateral travel with no lean readable at that distance.
- **Close-ups.** In `crops/high/R10-carrier` the carrier's torso is upright and the blockers hold identical half-squats. In `crops/high/R12-carrier` the ball is detached from the carrier in the pile.
- **Credits.** Three-point stances, a legible QB wind-up and a catch punch-in.
- **Verdict.** The 5 anchor ("cycles read, floaty contact, stiff falls") fits. Critic B's 4.5 leaned on the misread clock gap, so I rule 5.0, with Critic A and the advocate.

### UI / HUD: 6.5
- **Desktop is close to the 7 anchor.** `high/desktop/R04` has a scorebug, numbered and shape-coded receiver tags, route art and an on-field "1ST & 10". `high/desktop/R21` has a two-line result band with hierarchy that sits clear of the play.
- **Against the 7 anchor ("nothing stale or overlapping"):**
  - Bands overlap the play on the phone (`high/mobile/R12`, `high/landscape/R14`).
  - The third timeout dot is clipped in `high/landscape/R04` and `high/landscape/R14`.
  - The scorebug clock disagrees with the board and the log (`high/desktop/R12`).
  - The rail is mostly empty at R01 and R12.
  - The phone shows keyboard hints such as "TIMEOUT (T)" (`high/mobile/R12`).
- **Verdict.** All three reviewers agree on 6.5. Hold.

### Typography: 6.5
- **What works.** The condensed display and sans pairing is consistent, the hierarchy is clear and the numerals are strong on every viewport (`high/desktop/R21`, `high/mobile/R21`, `high/landscape/R14`).
- **What keeps it off 7.** Critic A's two cited causes were wrong (see above), but the anchor's "nothing clipped" still fails on screen. The tenth play card's title, "Fountain Play-Action", is cut by the viewport edge with no scroll cue (`high/desktop/R22`; the critics also cite `high/desktop/R03`). The secondary copy is small, low-contrast grey on navy: the rail description in `high/desktop/R01` and the log lines in `high/desktop/R21`.
- **Benchmark.** `legendbowl-4.jpg` has no small, low-contrast tier.
- **Verdict.** This is a cheap fix (gap 2), so I rule 6.5, with Critic A, not Critic B or the advocate.

### Readability: 6.0
- **Desktop.** The carrier ring and "44 DELACROIX" tag read (`high/desktop/R18`). The ball reads over turf (`high/desktop/R08`). The line of scrimmage and the first-down line are clear on every frame.
- **Phone.**
  - In `high/mobile/R12` the camera shows empty field while the pile sits under the band.
  - In `high/mobile/R04` receiver icons 1 and 2 are cut by the screen edges, and route art dwarfs players about 15 CSS px tall.
- **Verdict.** The 7 anchor says "every frame, both viewports", and the phone fails it. I rule 6.0, with Critic B and the advocate.

### Mobile: 5.5
- **Portrait framing.** In `high/mobile/R04` the top ~30% of the field view is dark stadium and scoreboard. The formation is small near the bottom, and the edge icons are clipped.
- **Bands.** They hide the play in `high/mobile/R12` and `high/mobile/R21`.
- **Landscape.** The rail takes about 42% of the width (`high/landscape/R04`), and the touchdown banner covers the celebration (`high/landscape/R14`).
- **What works.** The thumb targets are large.
- **Performance.** On the 4× throttle proxy, perf.json shows Medium (the phone tier) at 89 min fps and High at 44 min fps with a 34.4 ms p95, so performance is not the blocker.
- **Verdict.** All three reviewers agree on 5.5. Hold.

### Authenticity: 6.0 (gate met)
- **What works.** The scoreboard is an authentic high-school lamp board (`crops/high/R01-board`). `high/desktop/R20` has the Jessieville pines, small bleachers and a purple midfield.
- **What fails.** The establishing shot `high/desktop/R01` is a dark, empty, oversized bowl, with the HUD covering the stadium board. The sideline is a row of identical standing figures (`high/desktop/R13`). There is no band, cheer squad or student section.
- **Verdict.** All three reviewers agree on 6.0. Hold.

**Total: 47.5.** Only authenticity meets its gate.

## Ranked gaps (expected gain ÷ effort; S=1, M=2, L=3)

Ties and near-ties go to categories still below their gate.

1. **G-phone-bands-cover-play** (mobile, WS7). S, severity 4, +0.5; also helps readability.
   - On phone viewports, dock result and touchdown bands to the top edge of the field view, or re-aim the post-play camera so the pile's screen y sits above the band's top edge.
   - Cap the landscape touchdown banner at 25% of the field-view height.
   - Test: in `mobile/R12`, `mobile/R21` and `landscape/R14`, the carrier ring is fully visible.
2. **G-type-clip-contrast** (typography, WS7). S, severity 3, +0.5.
   - Give the play list a bottom fade and scroll cue, or compact the cards so all 10 fit at 800 px.
   - Raise the rail description, log and band-subtitle text to at least 4.5:1 contrast and at least 13 px.
   - Test: no glyph is cut at any container edge in R03 or R22.
3. **G-mesh-tears-gloves-facemask** (art, WS3). S, severity 4, +0.4.
   - Weld or merge the sleeve-hem and stripe geometry that breaks into sawteeth.
   - Shorten the glove fingers and curl them into a relaxed fist.
   - Seat the facemask on the shell with side clips.
   - Test: `crops/high/R06-slot_C-side` and `R06-slot_LG`, and the raised hands in `high/desktop/R14`.
4. **G-phone-framing** (mobile, WS7). M, severity 4, +0.6.
   - In portrait pre-snap, pitch down so stadium, sky and scoreboard take at most 15% of the field view.
   - Clamp receiver icons inside a 24 px safe area, and scale the formation about 1.3×.
   - In landscape, collapse the rail to the HIKE button plus a mini diagram during the live play, so the field gets at least 70% of the width.
5. **G-establishing-shot** (authenticity, WS7). S, severity 3, +0.3.
   - Re-aim the R01 kickoff camera to frame the lit home stands, crowd and scoreboard.
   - Drop the HUD-covered board, or push the camera so the board sits below the scorebug.
6. **G-rain-surface** (lighting, WS5). S, severity 3, +0.3.
   - In rain, darken the turf albedo about 20% and lower its roughness so lamp reflections streak at gameplay distance.
   - Replace the high-frequency sparkle noise with broad, low-frequency puddle sheen.
   - Make the square splash quads round, soft-alpha sprites.
7. **G-hud-consistency** (ui, WS7). S, severity 2, +0.3.
   - Drive the scorebug, board and log clocks from one value, so 3:18 vs 3:17 cannot happen.
   - Fit the timeout dots under 900 px by dropping the HOME tag.
   - Hide keyboard hints such as "(T)" and "Space" on touch devices.
8. **G-tackle-catch-beats** (animation, WS4b). L, severity 5, +0.75.
   - Add a hands-to-ball catch reach keyed 6–8 ticks before arrival.
   - Blend a 3–4 pose stumble-and-fall after contact.
   - Parent the ball to the carrier's hand bone through the fall.
   - Add body-separation offsets in piles.
   - Show a drive-and-fall in the sideline replay.
   - Capture the R09, R10 and R13 strips at even tick intervals across the catch and the fall, so the beats are verifiable.
9. **G-pants-silhouette** (art, WS3). M, severity 4, +0.5.
   - Cut the glute and hip volume about 30% and taper into the thigh.
   - Add thigh and knee pad planes.
   - Re-weight the deep-stance seat.
   - Add OL, skill and QB body scales.
   - Test: `crops/high/R04-slot_QB`, `R10-carrier`.
10. **G-replay-smudge** (lighting, WS1). S, severity 2, +0.2.
    - Remove or cull the near-camera occluder that draws the dark vertical bar at bottom centre of the sideline replay (`high/desktop/R13`, `strips/high/R13`).
11. **G-run-play-body-language** (animation, WS4). L, severity 4, +0.6.
    - Carrier: forward torso lean in proportion to speed, lateral lean plus a plant frame on cuts.
    - Blockers: an engage-and-drive cycle (hands extended, a step, a torso response) instead of a held half-squat.
    - Test: `crops/high/R10-carrier`, `R11-carrier`.
12. **G-officials-model** (art, WS3). M, severity 3, +0.3.
    - Move the officials onto the new body, with attached striped sleeves, a cap and a face.
    - Test: `crops/high/R03-slot_QB`.

Just below the cut:
- G-venue-pageantry: band, student section and cheer. L, +0.5. Authenticity is already at its gate.
- G-desktop-rail: M, +0.3.
- G-ball-visibility: dropped. The ball reads over turf in all three R08 frames.

## Verdict

**Continue.** Seven of the eight gates are unmet. Only authenticity (6.0 against a gate of 6) passes.
