Changes since loop 3 (facts only). Score **High** on desktop. Medium is the phone and mid tier. Low is the v2 look, captured to catch regressions.

- **Jersey numbers (all tiers).** The number panel now draws only the digits and their outline. The panel no longer shows a jersey-colored patch rectangle around them.
- **Facemasks.** A team with a dark helmet gets a light grey facemask, so the bars read against the helmet and the face. Other teams keep their dark mask.
- **Phone result toast.** On narrow screens the result banner is an opaque band docked along the bottom edge of the field, so it no longer floats over the play.
- **Camera punch-in (live, not with reduced motion).** For a beat after a catch or a hit, the live camera tightens by up to 25% over 0.9 s, then eases back out.
- **Unchanged since loop 3:** player and crowd assets, lighting setup, post chain, HUD layout and type, venue geometry, animation clips.
