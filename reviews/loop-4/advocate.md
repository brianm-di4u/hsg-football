# Advocate: loop 4, High tier, desktop

I opened every capture cited below. For a few claims I made enlarged crops or sampled pixels from the same PNGs; where I did, I say what I measured. All paths are relative to `games/football/screenshots/review-loop-4/`.

## Summary

Most of what the critics say about players and motion holds up. I concede both art scores, the authenticity scores and the typography scores. The upside-down chest numbers are worse than Critic A argued, not better: Lakeside's **#90 reads correctly standing upright in `crops/high/R03-slot_QB` and reads "06" once he is in stance in `crops/high/R04-slot_C`.** That is the same player in two stances, which confirms a stance-dependent flip.

I contest four things.
1. **Lighting.** Critic A's "stands void" case rests on wide shots that frame the stands out. Critic A also says High has no lamp heads, which is false. Critic B says there is a single dominant shadow direction, and R01 and R17 contradict that.
2. **The R16 "conflicting instructions" claim** (UI). The two strings describe the same green zone.
3. **The official model.** It has a brown skin tone separate from the black clothing, and the referee wears a white cap.
4. **Critic B's 4.5 for animation.** That score sits below an anchor the captures meet. Critic B also credits a camera tightening in `strips/high/R09` that isn't in the strip.

## Category by category

### Art (A 5.5, B 5.5 → proposed 5.5)

- **Concede: chest-number flip.** In `crops/high/R06-slot_C` (enlarged), the white #63 reads "Ɛ9". In `crops/high/R04-slot_C`, "53" reads "ƐS" and #90 reads "06". `crops/low/R04-slot_C` shows the same thing. Standing players read correctly (`crops/high/R03-slot_QB`: "90", "96").
- **Concede: tube limbs, mitten hands, no cloth folds.** See `crops/high/R06-slot_C`, `crops/high/R04-slot_QB` and `crops/high/R14-carrier`.
- **Concede: floating official sleeves.** In `crops/high/R03-slot_QB` and `crops/high/R12-carrier`, the striped sleeve boxes have a visible gap at the shoulder.
- **Rebut A (plainly wrong): official model.** A calls the official "a featureless black mannequin" and asks for skin-tone faces, white caps and skin "separate from black clothing". Enlarged crops of `crops/high/R04-slot_QB`, `crops/high/R12-carrier` and `crops/high/R04-slot_C` show brown skin-tone heads, necks and forearms, distinct from the black pants, under a black cap. In `crops/high/R03-slot_QB` the referee wears a **white cap**. Only the lack of facial features stands.
- **Partial rebut: crowd repetition (B).** `crops/high/R01-crowd` shows mixed shirt colors (orange, black, white) and mixed poses (two arms up, one arm up, arms crossed, arms down). It is repetitive in body shape but not in pose. This doesn't change the score.
- Both critics agree the build clears the 5 anchor: helmet gloss against the matte jersey (`crops/high/R06-slot_LG`), a crowd that reads as people, and turf grain. Both also agree it misses 7 on anatomy and gear. With a visible decal bug added, 5.5 is fair.

### Lighting & atmosphere (A 6.0, B 6.5 → proposed 6.5)

- **Rebut A: wrong cause for the "stands void".** `high/desktop/R01` and `high/desktop/R17` are steep top-down end-zone shots. The bleachers run along the sidelines and fall outside the frame. I brightened the top band of R01 4× (mean luminance about 27/255). What is there is the end-zone wall and apron, plus a thin line of spectator specks at the far edge, not a stand that is lit too dimly.
  - Wherever the stands are in frame, they are lit and populated: `crops/high/R01-crowd`, the left edge of `high/desktop/R04`, `high/desktop/R13`, `high/desktop/R15` and `high/desktop/R20` (both sides).
  - The real defect is an undressed, unlit perimeter: the wall and apron. Critic B words this correctly ("dull, largely featureless perimeter").
- **Rebut A (plainly wrong): lamp heads on High.** A's fix says to "add visible lamp heads on the poles in High (Medium already shows them)". In `high/desktop/R04` (crop of the top-left pole), High renders a lit lamp bank with individual lamps and bloom. The same banks appear in `high/desktop/R19`, `high/desktop/R20`, `high/desktop/R21` and `high/desktop/R22`. Medium shows the same fixture unlit, as a silhouette (`medium/desktop/R04`).
- **Rebut B: shadows.** B says the lighting shows "dominant directional shadows rather than the convincing overlapping light contribution expected from multiple stadium towers." An enlarged crop of `high/desktop/R17` shows each coverage player casting two soft shadow fans in opposite directions. `high/desktop/R01` shows the same cross-shadows under the kickoff line. This is the 7 anchor's "several soft shadows under players".
- **Concede:**
  - No wet sheen on players in rain (`crops/high/R18-turf_26_67_40`). The splash particles are flat white squares near the feet. A puts them at knee height; most of them sit at turf level.
  - The night sky is very saturated next to the dim perimeter (`high/desktop/R04`).
- What is visible against the 7 anchor:
  - bloom on the lamps (R04, R20)
  - multiple soft shadows (R01, R17)
  - wet sheen on the turf (`crops/high/R18-turf_26_67_40`)
  - a convincing day mist (`high/desktop/R19`)
  - a believable night exposure
- What is missing: falloff onto the perimeter structure. That earns 6.5, not 6.0.

**Critic A (critic-claude)**
1. R16 FG instructions "conflict". They name the same zone (`high/desktop/R16`).
2. Officials are "featureless black mannequins" needing skin-tone faces, white caps and separate skin. They have brown skin-tone heads and arms, and the referee wears a white cap (`crops/high/R03-slot_QB`, `crops/high/R04-slot_QB`, `crops/high/R12-carrier`).

5. The R12 carrier is "buried under the banner". The banner sits below the pile (`high/desktop/R12`).
6. Lake Hamilton's kit is "identical" to Hot Springs'. The hue differs on sampling (`high/desktop/R03` vs `high/desktop/R04`).
7. The celebration is "everyone doing the same arms-up pose". `crops/high/R14-carrier` shows one-arm, two-arm-with-ball and low-reach poses.

**Critic B (critic-codex)**
1. `strips/high/R09` "shows camera tightening". The framing is constant across all 8 panels.
2. The lighting shows "dominant directional shadows". Multi-shadow fans are visible in `high/desktop/R17` and `high/desktop/R01`.
3. R11 shows a "relatively upright posture". `crops/high/R11-carrier` shows a forward-pitched torso.
