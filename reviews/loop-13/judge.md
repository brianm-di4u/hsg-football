All capture paths are relative to `games/football/screenshots/review-loop-13/`. In every category the two critics are within 1.0 of each other, so no category needed a forced arbitration. Where the critics and the advocate disagreed on a fact, I opened the captures myself, and I say below who was right.

**Verdict: continue.** Art, lighting, animation, UI, readability and mobile are all below their 7 gate. Typography is at its gate and authenticity is at its gate of 6.

## Category rulings

### Art: 6.0 (critics 6.0 / 6.0, advocate 6.0)
- **What reaches the 7 anchor:** glossy, hot-specular helmets (including copper metallic ones) against matte jerseys, modeled facemasks, clean stripe trim and fingered hands (`crops/high/R06-slot_C-side`, `crops/high/R03-slot_QB`). Turf grain holds at field level, and the crowd reads as people (`crops/high/R01-crowd`).
- **What holds it below 7:**
  - There are no facial features under the facemasks (`crops/high/R14-carrier`).
  - Every position shares one build. Shoulder shells are inflated and waists pinched (`crops/high/R06-slot_C-side`, `crops/high/R03-slot_QB` official).
  - Pants balloon in stance (`crops/high/R04-slot_C` #69), and jerseys have no cloth folds.
  - Arm and glove are one color with no skin break.
  - Off the hero angle the venue is thin: cone trees and a flat wall (`high/desktop/R15`).
- Against axis-3 it is clearly below 7 and clearly above the palette-only 5. **6.0.**

### Lighting & atmosphere: 6.5 (6.5 / 6.5, advocate 6.5)
- **For:** lamp bloom and a believable night exposure (`high/desktop/R01`, `high/desktop/R20`). Two or three soft shadows fan from every player (`crops/high/R18-turf_26_67_40`, `high/desktop/R17`). Helmet speculars are hot.
- **Against:**
  - Rain doesn't read as wet at play distance (`high/desktop/R18` vs `high/desktop/R10`), so the anchor's "wet sheen in rain" is met only in the close-up.
  - The replay backdrop is a flat, unlit wall (`high/desktop/R15`).
  - There is no haze in the lamp cones.
  - The bleacher back-row falloff is present but modest (`crops/high/R01-crowd`).
- Near cfb26-3 and axis-3, not at them. **6.5.**

### Animation & game feel: 5.5 (5.5 / 5.0, advocate 5.5)
- **Real contact exists:** `strips/high/R13` frames 5–8 show an attached defender and a tip-over, and `crops/high/R12-carrier` a grounded two-body pile. Codex's "no wrap" is overstated.
- **But:**
  - The dive stop is an upright squatting cluster with no fall (`strips/high/R10`).
  - The catch cuts straight to a static result with no run-after-catch tackle (`strips/high/R09` frames 6–8).
  - Celebrants repeat one or two poses (`crops/high/R14-carrier`).
  - The carrier's ball isn't visible on the camera side (`crops/high/R13-carrier`).
  - The slow-motion catch can't be credited from stills.
- That is a 5 ("stiff falls") lifted half a point by one real fall and pile. **5.5.**

### UI / HUD: 6.5 (6.5 / 6.5, advocate 6.5)
- **For:** numbered, shaped receiver badges, dashed route art and a matching panel diagram (`high/desktop/R04`). Clear two-line result plates (`high/desktop/R21`). Carrier ring and name plate (`high/desktop/R10`, `high/desktop/R15`).
- **Against:**
  - Defense pre-snap has nothing on the field. The Fire Blitz exists only in the panel thumbnail (`high/desktop/R05`).
  - The desktop plate sits over the end of the play (`high/desktop/R12`).
  - The right panel is mostly empty below its buttons (`high/desktop/R04`, `high/desktop/R05`).
- **6.5.**

### Typography: 7.0 (6.5 / 7.0, advocate 7.0)
- Both of Critic A's main typography faults turned out to be wrong (see the fact table).
- What I see is one bold condensed display face for team names, the clock, plate titles, buttons and play titles, plus one humanist sans for descriptions and the log. The sizes step clearly: plate title, then subline, then panel head, then body (`high/desktop/R21`, `high/desktop/R22`, `medium/mobile/R22`).
- Numerals read at a glance, and I found nothing clipped (`high/desktop/R04`, `medium/mobile/R04`).
- **Remaining misses:** down and distance in mixed case below the clock size, the roughly 10 px "HOME" tag, and the tiny play thumbnails on desktop.
- This meets the madden26-3 / legendbowl-4 anchor at its floor, not beyond. **7.0** (confidence moderate).

### Readability: 6.5 (6.5 / 6.0, advocate 6.5)
- **For:** in every live frame I opened, the carrier has a ring and a name plate, the LOS is blue and the line to gain yellow, and targets carry badges (`high/desktop/R10`, `high/desktop/R18`, `medium/landscape/R10`, `medium/mobile/R04`).
- **Against:**
  - Bodies are small on desktop, with about two-thirds of `high/desktop/R04` given to sky and venue.
  - The dive carrier is buried among black jerseys, at the bottom edge under his tag (`high/desktop/R10`).
  - Defenders are about 25 px and unlabeled (`high/desktop/R05`).
  - In `high/desktop/R12` the downed carrier is hidden at the result.
- Codex's "wide targets offscreen" in portrait is handled by deliberate edge badges with arrows (`medium/mobile/R04`), so that point doesn't cost the full half point. **6.5.**

### Mobile: 6.0 (6.0 / 6.0, advocate 6.0)
- **Landscape is now intentional:** the carrier and plate sit well up, and the JUKE button is large (`medium/landscape/R10`).
- **Portrait still falls short:**
  - About 55% of the field view above the formation is empty turf, and the linemen are about 36–40 device px (`medium/mobile/R04`).
  - Badge 1 sits on the "1ST & 10" paint (`medium/mobile/R04`).
  - The play-call screen shows six of ten cards with no scroll cue (`medium/mobile/R22`).
- **Perf:** Medium, the phone tier, holds 40 fps minimum under the 4× throttle, which meets the anchor's 30 fps. Its 71.4 ms worst p95 frame is a real hitch tail.
- Below retrobowl-1 and retrobowl-2 on figure scale. **6.0.**

### Authenticity: 6.0 (6.0 / 6.5, advocate 6.5)
- **For:** an HS scoreboard with DOWN / TO GO / BALL ON (`high/desktop/R01`), local schools and play names, three distinct venues including a 3A Jessieville (`high/desktop/R20`, `crops/high/R03-slot_QB` fieldhouse), benches lining the sidelines, and an H-style goalpost.
- **Against:** none of the 7 anchor's people items appear: no band, no student section, no cheer squad (`crops/high/R01-crowd`). The track is a plain strip.
- **Off-angle staging breaks the small-town illusion:**
  - In `high/desktop/R15`, a two-section visitor bleacher stands in front of a flat wall, under cone trees taller than the light poles, with the visitor team in a parade-ground row.
  - In `high/desktop/R17`, the field is a slab on a dark plane.
- The venue variety the advocate cites is real, but the off-angle views cancel it out. **6.0**: at the gate, not above it.

## Ranked gaps (gain ÷ effort, top 12, duplicates merged)

1. **G-ui-defense-presnap** (ui, WS7, S, +0.5). Fix: draw the called blitz and coverage on the turf (red rush arrows from each blitzer, shaded zone ovals), ring the user-controlled defender, and tag the two biggest offensive threats with badges.
2. **G-mobile-portrait-scale**, merging G-formation-framing (mobile, WS7, M, +0.75). Fix:
   - Put the portrait LOS at 55–60% of the field-view height and zoom until the OL are at least 26 CSS px. Keep the edge badges for out-of-frame WRs.
   - Move the badge off the "1ST & 10" paint.
   - Add a peeking seventh card or a scroll cue on the play-call screen.
3. **G-anim-contact-falls**, merging G-contact (animation, WS4b, M, +0.75). The cause is corrected: the wrap and fall set already exists (R12, R13). What is missing is that it doesn't fire on interior pile stops (R10) or after a catch (R09). Fix: route every whistle through the wrap and fall, add one more fall variant (side twist), and put two or three nearby defenders on the ground before the result plate.
4. **G-art-player-identity**, merging G-player-anatomy (art, WS3, L, +0.75). Fix:
   - Skin-toned forearms and wrists between the sleeve and a separate glove.
   - Brow, nose and jaw shading on the head under the facemask.
   - Per-position body scale: OL and DL 1.15× wider in the torso and hips, WR and DB leaner.
   - Shoulder pads blended into the torso instead of inflated shells.
   - Pants that don't balloon in the three-point stance.
   - A normal-mapped jersey fold and tuck layer.
5. **G-venue-offangle-void** (authenticity, WS2, M, +0.5). Fix:
   - Replace the flat wall behind the replay angle with a full visitor bleacher run and a press box.
   - Cap the cone trees at the light-pole height and push them behind the stands.
   - Dress both end zones with a chain-link fence, a track bend and a lit berm, so that R14, R15 and R17 show built objects.
6. **G-auth-band-student**, merging the overlapping part of G-crowd-repetition (authenticity, WS2, M, +0.5). Fix:
   - A uniformed band block, with instruments, in one home-stand section.
   - A student section in team-color shirts with signs.
   - A cheer squad and a mascot on the track.
   - Crowd height and build variance of ±10%, with seated clusters that break the grid.
7. **G-rain-sheen-readable** (lighting, WS5, M, +0.5). Fix: view-dependent, elongated lamp-bank reflections on wet turf toward the camera, visible in `high/desktop/R18` at play distance. Add rain streaks brightened inside the lamp cones, and enlarge the existing cleat splash flecks.
8. **G-lighting-falloff-haze**, merging G-atmospheric-depth (lighting, WS1, M, +0.5). Fix:
   - A soft fog cone under each lamp bank at night.
   - Top bleacher rows at 40% or less of the front-row luminance.
   - Spill light on the walls behind the stands.
   - A depth-weighted day mist, so the R19 background keeps its silhouettes.
9. **G-ui-plate-occludes**, absorbing the empty-panel note (ui, WS7, S, +0.25). Fix: on desktop, move the result plate into the empty right panel or a lower-third strip of 60 px or less so the pile stays visible. Also remove the duplicate "FIELD GOAL · 45 YD" title from R16.
10. **G-typo-down-distance** (typography, WS7, S, +0.25). Fix: set down and distance in all caps at the clock's size in its own scorebug cell, and raise the "HOME" tag to at least 12 px. The face is already correct.
11. **G-weight-and-catch** (animation, WS4, L, +0.5). Fix: a planted cut step with torso counter-rotation on sweeps, a visible catch-to-tuck transition, and the ball carried on the camera-side arm.
12. **G-perf-phone-hitch**, merging G-phone-frame-time (mobile, WS7, M, +0.25). Fix: profile the 71.4 ms p95 spikes in Medium phone replay under the 4× throttle (crowd matrix updates, GC, shader warm-up). Impostor or LOD the crowd on the phone tier, with a target p95 of 40 ms or less.
